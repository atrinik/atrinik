# Nawerhals

Nawerhals was originally authored by Daniel Liptrot and maintained in the
archived `atrinik/nawerhals` repository. Historical repository setup and
maintenance contributions by Zoey Rose are credited under her current legal
name.

The maps were ported into the Atrinik monorepo and updated to use the current
map-naming, archetype, artifact, and region conventions in August 2026. Their
surface occupies the Strakewood world grid from `world_-10_50` through
`world_-4_61`, with a bridge to Strakewood's western coast at
`world_-4_57`. Muddy Pit and Glacial Hollow use matching `world_*` underground
coordinates below their surface entrances.
